FORMATIVE TESTING

Testing Plan

Objective

Evaluate the usability of 6 lo-fi paper prototypes through formative testing before developing the hi-fi prototype.

Target Participants

3 participants (18-25 years old, university students) with no prior knowledge of the prototypes. Chess followers and mobile web users.

Testing Method

Paper Prototype Testing using Think-Aloud Protocol. Systematically collecting qualitative observations & feedback.

Qualitative Data Collected

• Behavioral Observations: Real-time user interactions & hesitations.
• User Feedback: Subjective opinions on clarity & navigation.
• Issues & Improvements: Points of friction identified for PA4.

FORMATIVE QUALITATIVE STUDY

Task Behavioral Observations

01 · TASK 1 OBSERVATIONS Navigation Bar Behaviors
• Bottom Nav (V1): Natural thumb reach while walking with zero phone-grip shifting; immediate 1-tap reaction.
• FAB (V2): Noticeable hesitation locating menu before tapping.
• Bottom Hamburger (V3): Full-screen overlay disoriented users and caused context loss.
02 · TASK 2 OBSERVATIONS Schedule Lookup Behaviors
• Accordion Cards (V1): Users immediately understood the ▼ chevron for instant inline player lookups without reloading.
• Date Filter (V2): Loved player analysis on detail view, but multi-step dropdowns added friction.
• Timeline (V3): Users had to scroll up multiple times to locate past tournaments/events in chronological order.

User Feedback & Usability Breakdowns

03 · USER FEEDBACK Paraphrased Quotes
• Participant 1 (T1-V1): "Bottom Nav is effortless, my thumb rests right there."

04 · KEY BREAKDOWNS Usability Issues Identified
• Discoverability Issue: FAB hides menu items until tapped, causing trial-and-error.
• Context Loss: Full-screen overlay breaks page continuity with current reading screen.
• Overloading Information: Too much information shown while expanding.
• Lack of Searching Operation: Users have to scroll many times to reach past events.

CONCLUSION & NEXT STEPS

Best Variant Selection

SELECTED VARIANT · TASK 1

Variant 1: Fixed Bottom Nav Bar

• 1-Tap Instant Reach: Zero hierarchical menus needed; instant 1-handed completion.
• Optimal Thumb Zone: Eliminates phone slip risk while walking on campus.
• Zero Cognitive Load: Primary sections (Schedule, Videos, News) always visible.
SELECTED VARIANT · TASK 2

Variant 2: Date Filter + Detail Page

• Rapid Date Lookup: Dropdown filters for Month & Year + interactive date strip for quick searching.
• Rich Dedicated Match Details: "Detail →" button reveals full match view with deep player analysis.
• Intuitive Drill-Down: Clear separation between quick date selection and deep game analysis.
IMPROVEMENTS IDENTIFIED FOR PA4 (HI-FI PROTOTYPE)
1. Touch Target Compliance
Ensure all nav items, buttons, and card chevrons strictly meet minimum 48×48px touch targets (WCAG standards).
2. Motion & Active Feedback
Implement smooth accordion expand/collapse transitions and clear active tab highlight indicators.
3. Quick Search & Category Filters
Add instant player search bar and tournament category filter tags (Open, Rapid, Blitz) for rapid lookup.
