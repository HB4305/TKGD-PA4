## B. Requirements

### Requirement 1: High-fidelity prototype
Your group chooses the best versions of the lo-fi prototypes in the previous PA and develops it into the hi-fi prototype (based on the formative testing results). If you are not satisfied with any of those lo-fi prototypes as each has its pros and cons, you can develop a new design based on the explored approaches and build a hi-fi prototype for it. To illustrate the interface, user flow, and user interactions of your solutions, you can use several prototyping tools such as Figma, Adobe Illustrator, Powerpoint or vibe coding... or implement it by writing code (creating a script for demonstration and not requiring dynamic data for this prototype).

You should consider several aspects of the high-fidelity prototype which is different from the paper prototype (low-fidelity prototype):
- **Rich fidelity:** detailed color, UI elements, animations...
- **Realistic user interactions** (touch, mouse, keyboard, ...)
- **Content:** you should use real or similar-to-real content. Please ensure the data should make sense for the chosen contexts.

> *The hi-fi prototype task must be completed before the presentation/demo date.*

---

### Requirement 2: Summative User Study
Your group is asked to plan and conduct a user study to evaluate your solution (hi-fi prototype). The requirements for this PA:

- **Before the User study:**
  - Create a plan in details for your study: setups, procedure, questionnaires, and study designs.
  - Invite at least 5 participants to test your prototype.
  - Tasks of each team member.
  - The measures you use to evaluate your prototype.
- **In progress:**
  - Video-record all testing sessions.
- **After testing:**
  - Gather feedback (through interview), insights or questionnaire answer from the participants.
  - Collect other data (Ex: the time participant completed the specific task using your prototype and compare it to the current practice).

> *The user study task must be completed before the presentation/demo date.*
